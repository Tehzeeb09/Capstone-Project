Football, known as soccer in some parts of the world, is the world's most popular sport, captivating billions of fans across the globe. The game is played between two teams of eleven players each, who use their feet, torso, and head to advance a spherical ball down a rectangular field. The primary objective is simple yet thrilling: to navigate past the opposing team's defense and kick or head the ball into their goal net.

Except for the goalkeeper, players are strictly prohibited from using their hands or arms during active play, creating a unique dynamic that emphasizes exceptional footwork, speed, and spatial awareness.At its core, football is a beautiful blend of individual artistry and collective strategy. Matches are split into two 45-minute halves, during which teams continuously transition between tactical defensive formations and explosive attacking plays. Success on the pitch demands seamless communication, precise passing sequences, and intense physical conditioning.
